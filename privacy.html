Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

GTA 6 Clips is a personal publishing tool operated by a single individual for their own social media accounts.

Data we access

When you authorize the app, it receives an access token for your account and basic profile information (account ID and username). It uses these only to upload videos you have chosen to publish and to check their upload status.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deletion

You can revoke access at any time from your account settings on each platform. To request deletion of any stored data, contact miosgta6clips@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.
